§ 01 — About

Why a biologist
builds vision models.

I started in biology (BSc, Nazarbayev University) and moved into machine learning through my MSc at Eötvös Loránd University, where I built an automated microscope that scans slides and detects fossil pollen live. That background shapes how I work: I know what the expert on the other side of the model needs, and I check results against them rather than against a leaderboard.

Right now I am a research assistant at Nazarbayev University School of Medicine, building a deep-learning pipeline that screens routine lumbar-spine CT for osteoporosis and sarcopenia.

A thread through all of my projects: evaluation that holds up. Leakage-free splits, comparisons to expert counts on independent material, statistical tests, and reporting the negative results too.

§ 03 — Lab notebook

Where I've worked.

  1. Aug 2026 — now

    Research Assistant · AI for Clinical CT Imaging

    School of Medicine, Nazarbayev University · PI Dr. Srinivasa Bolla

    • Deep-learning pipeline for opportunistic osteoporosis and sarcopenia screening from routine lumbar-spine CT, linking L3 psoas biomarkers to DXA bone mineral density in 280 patients.
    • Automated vertebra and psoas segmentation on a multi-vendor cohort (5 manufacturers): baseline mean Dice 0.89 against expert-corrected masks.
  2. Sep 2025 — Jul 2026

    Research Assistant · Automated Microscopy & Image Analysis

    Palaeoecology Research Group, Eötvös Loránd University · PI Dr. Enikő Magyari

    • Automated microscope-scanning and live-detection workflow replacing manual slide counting (75% faster).
    • Released a 22-class detection dataset (3,001 images, 8,460 boxes); benchmarked 7 CNN / transformer detectors across 21 runs under a leakage-free depth-based split.
    • Trained a ResNet50 classifier for subfossil chironomids on 27,774 specimen images from 57 lakes in 5 countries.
  3. May — Aug 2025

    Junior ML Engineer · Video Behaviour Recognition

    Green Eye Ltd

    • Built a 6-class behaviour dataset (~20K labelled frames / clips); benchmarked 10+ CV approaches and 15+ transfer-learning configurations by precision, recall, macro-F1 and confusion matrices.
